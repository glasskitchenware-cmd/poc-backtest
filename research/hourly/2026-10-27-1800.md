## VERIFIED: all-reject mechanism and diagnostic discrimination

The stored logistic-calibrated `predictedProfitProbability` lies **0.32955–0.37264** on test (mean 0.35895), so thresholds 0.50/0.55/0.60 mathematically reject all 2,835. This is **not** a missing-value bug in the stored scores: all are finite and matched to trades. The saved `logistic.joblib` calibrator has a negative slope `-0.04048647` on base-model log odds; `hist_classifier.joblib` has slope `-0.33706394`. A negative slope is not by itself proof of a code defect, but the compressed scores and weak rank performance require review.

- Test AUC **0.4995**; Brier **0.23294**, slightly worse than constant observed-base-rate Brier **0.23283**. The score does not meaningfully distinguish winners from losers in this period.
- Five equal-count test score quintiles (567 trades each), ascending predicted probability, have mean net R **[-0.2090, -0.2302, -0.1985, -0.2090, -0.2253]**. Even the highest-score quintile loses. These are descriptive bins, **not a candidate selection or untouched OOS**.
- Test realized mean winning trade **+0.9069R** and mean losing trade **-0.8700R** imply a descriptive break-even win rate of **48.96%** under these realized conditional payoffs; the actual win rate is **36.90%**. This does not license a universal probability threshold, because conditional payoff sizes vary by signal.
- All-reject avoids 1,789 losing/nonpositive trades and sacrifices 1,046 winners; avoided negative R **1,556.377**, discarded positive R **948.590**. Retained 0 trades, realized trading profit **0**, no demonstrated filter edge.

## EXPLORATORY / correctness limits

- Filter experiment kept original POC entry and SL/TP. `research/futures_filter/run.py` trains on 2023 Jan–Sep, calibrates Oct–Dec, evaluates 2024 and 2025–Sep2026. The latter was **previously researched** and is not a globally untouched holdout. Model AUC on 2024 0.5174 and 2025–Sep2026 0.4995 shows no convincing ranking edge.
- POC `flow_breakout` execution uses adverse slippage in fill prices and derives `slipR=gross-price`; it reconciles exactly. **Do not transfer the separate `web/trend.js` double-slippage finding to this POC engine.**

Actual funding is a scenario, not signed exchange settlement. The current ledger cannot establish margin, liquidation, tick-order ambiguity or real-money viability.
- The 2024 validation is not a fresh untouched holdout; selecting an attractive 2025 score band after seeing this audit would be post-hoc. No revised filter was promoted.
